Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Signature Tow Support ("we," "us," or "our"), a service operated by Whatif Multimedia Productions, Inc., respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to signaturetow.com, text2tow.com, and our SMS/text messaging programs.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you provide — such as your name, business name, mobile phone number, and email address — when you sign up for our service, request a tow, or contact us.
  • Service information related to tow and roadside requests, such as vehicle details, location, and the details of calls handled on your behalf.
  • Usage data such as pages visited and basic device information, collected through standard web analytics.

How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the service — answering calls, dispatching tows, and sending you related notifications.
  • To send you SMS/text messages you have consented to receive, such as job alerts and booking or status confirmations.
  • To respond to your requests and provide customer support.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

SMS / text messaging

By providing your mobile number and opting in, you consent to receive SMS messages from Signature Tow Support and Text2Tow related to the service you requested or subscribed to. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and reply HELP for assistance.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never sold or shared. Information may be shared only with subcontractors who directly support the service (for example, a messaging provider), and only as needed to deliver the messages you requested. All other categories of data exclude text-messaging opt-in data; that information is not shared with any third parties.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only: (a) with service providers who help us operate (such as our telephony/SMS and payment providers), under confidentiality obligations; (b) when required by law; or (c) to protect the safety and rights of our users and the public. As stated above, mobile opt-in data is never shared for marketing.

Data retention & security

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal requirements, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the details below.

Children's privacy

Our service is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
